# Simmer Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Simmer is a free recipe app. Here is everything it does with data.

**What stays on your phone.** Your recipes, grocery list, pantry items, and giving stats are stored only on your device. Simmer has no account system and no server of its own. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

**What leaves your phone.**
- When you import a recipe, Simmer downloads that web page directly from the site you pasted. The site sees a normal page request from your phone.
- Recipe photos are loaded from the site that published them.
- The "Watch to feed" feature shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your advertising ID and device information to serve and measure ads, under Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
- Donation buttons open the charity's own website in your browser. Simmer never handles payment details.

**What Simmer does not do.** No analytics, no tracking across apps, no selling of data, no email collection.

**Children.** Simmer is not directed at children under 13.

**Contact.** Questions go to the project page: https://github.com/justinrayandrews1984-hub/Simmer
